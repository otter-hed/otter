# Otter states for the Schörner et al. (2022) aluminium comparison

These four archives were generated by the complete gallery program
`benchmarks/examples/plot_schorner_et_al_2022_al_sii.py` and refreshed with
the September 2026 Otter campaign. Historical MD is retained separately.
They contain project-generated equilibrium structure factors using both
LDA-PW92 (`lda_pw`) and PBE (`pbe`) for:

- Al, `rho = 4.712 g/cc`, `Te = Ti = 1 eV`;
- Al, `rho = 8.1 g/cc`, `Te = Ti = 5 eV`.

For every state/XC case, ordinary HNC and Rosenfeld--Ashcroft VMHNC reuse the
same IS electronic result and QOZ pair potential.  Each archive also contains
a historical 2048-ion LAMMPS calculation with the earlier potential,
explicitly flagged `md_is_historical=True`. The MD protocol is
`NVT -> NVE`, with timestep `0.005 omega_p^-1`, `50 omega_p^-1`
equilibration, and `500 omega_p^-1` production.  All four relative NVE energy
drifts are below `2.5e-6`.

The MD `Sii(k)` is evaluated directly from periodic density modes and averaged
over every available half-space reciprocal vector in each radial bin.  No
random directional cap is applied.  The line averages 21 saved NVE frames
(the initial production frame plus 20 production intervals), and the retained
uncertainty is the SEM across the shell-averaged frames.  It is not obtained
from a truncated RDF transform. Comparison errors are regenerated from the
current arrays. Historical MD is not used to claim validation of the refreshed
HNC closure at an identical pair potential.

The states pass full-AA stage-2, external-AA, threshold-state, HNC, VMHNC
variational, transform-closure, and MD audit gates. The retained arrays are
pickle-free and the closure arrays cover `k <= 20 Bohr^-1`.

The archives retain the Libxc 7.0.0 provider version, exact component IDs,
references, and DOIs. LDA means `lda_x + lda_c_pw`; PBE means
`gga_x_pbe + gga_c_pbe` and uses Otter's documented finite-core GGA
regularization.

VMHNC is a variational hard-sphere bridge and is not IEMHNC.  IEMHNC maps an
OCP bridge to a Yukawa one-component plasma (YOCP); the non-Yukawa PA-QOZ
potentials here are not given that label.

The literature data are stored separately under
`benchmarks/reference_data/schorner_et_al_2022_al_sii`. Its raw 1 eV ordinate
is corrected by `+1.5` only when loaded; the original CSV is never rewritten.
The reference package carries article attribution and license status
`NOASSERTION`. These Otter-generated baseline archives are project output.

With `USE_PRECOMPUTED_DATA = True`, the gallery validates the checksums below
before plotting. Set it to `False` to calculate fresh candidates under
`benchmarks/outputs/schorner_et_al_2022_al_sii/gallery_recomputed` without
silently replacing these accepted files.
